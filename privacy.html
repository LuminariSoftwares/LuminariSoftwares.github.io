Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026; TikTok and Instagram sections added November 1, 2026. Applies to the Luminari Studio app, which connects to the owner's own Google, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Who uses the app

The app is private software used only by the studio owner, Timothy Murphy, with the owner's own Google, TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no other person can sign in to it.

What Google data the app uses

  • Gmail: reads the owner's own incoming messages so they can be sorted into the studio's customer inbox, and creates reply drafts in the owner's Gmail. The app never sends email; the owner reviews and sends every message personally.
  • YouTube: reads view, like and comment counts for the owner's own channel's videos.

Where the data goes

Data from Google is stored only on computers the owner owns and controls. It is not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Email content is processed only by software and AI models running on the owner's own computers, not by outside AI services.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Luminari Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What TikTok data the app uses

  • Profile: the owner's own TikTok display name and follower count.
  • Videos: the list of the owner's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

The app does not post, comment, message or read anyone else's data on TikTok.

What Instagram data the app uses

  • Profile: the owner's own Instagram professional account name, follower count and post count.
  • Posts: the owner's own posts with their like and comment counts, and view or reach counts from Instagram insights.

The app does not post, comment, message or read anyone else's data on Instagram.

How TikTok and Instagram data is handled

These counts are stored only on computers the owner owns and controls, to track how the studio's own content performs. They are not sold, rented, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions; TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions; Instagram in Instagram or Facebook settings under apps and websites / business integrations. Stored data can be deleted on request.
